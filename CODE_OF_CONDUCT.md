
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience.

## Our Standards
We encourage respectful communication, constructive feedback, collaboration, and kindness.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and inappropriate comments.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and community interactions.

## Enforcement
Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers may issue a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent ban depending on the severity of the violation.

## Attribution
This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
